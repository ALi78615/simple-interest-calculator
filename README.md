Simple Interest CalculatorOverviewThis project is a lightweight, easy-to-use tool designed to calculate the simple interest earned or paid on a given principal amount over a specific time period. It is ideal for basic financial calculations, educational demonstrations, and simple programming practice.How Simple Interest WorksSimple interest is calculated only on the initial amount of money (the principal). Unlike compound interest, simple interest does not accumulate on previously earned interest.Mathematical Formula$$\text{Simple Interest (SI)} = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$$Where:$P$ (Principal): The initial amount of money deposited, borrowed, or invested.$R$ (Rate): The annual interest rate (expressed as a percentage).$T$ (Time): The time period the money is invested or borrowed for (expressed in years).Total Amount FormulaTo calculate the total accumulated value (Principal + Interest) after $T$ years:$$\text{Total Amount (A)} = P + \text{SI}$$FeaturesQuick Calculation: Instant evaluation of total interest based on user input.Flexible Usage: Supports custom values for principal, rate, and duration.Clear Output: Displays both the interest generated and the total final balance.Example CalculationSuppose you invest $1,000 ($P$) at an annual interest rate of 5% ($R$) for 3 years ($T$):$$\text{SI} = \frac{1000 \times 5 \times 3}{100} = \$150$$$$\text{Total Amount} = 1000 + 150 = \$1,150$$# simple-interest-calculator
